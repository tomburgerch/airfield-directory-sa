|-----|------------------|-------------------|-------|---------|---------|

**Inactive runways:** 03/21 (1,454 m × 90 m), 01/19 (1,080 m × 90 m)

**Notes:**
- Former SAAF base (WWII era, operated Lockheed Venturas)
- ~13 km NE of Durbanville
- ⚠️ **AIAA** (Area of Intense Aerial Activity) — banner tow, crop spraying, intensive flight training with multiple aircraft and multiple active runways
- Circuit restricted to 2.5 NM radius, all turns LEFT
- **Departure:** Maintain 1500 ft ALT until 5 NM, call outbound on 131.10
- **Transit:** Avoid overflying, maintain 2000 ft ALT from 5 NM until past field
- Tall mountain ranges to SE extending to 6,600 ft AMSL
- Prevailing wind: SE in summer, NW in winter. Mag var ~23°W
- Home to 4 Aviators flight training
- Close to FAD 200 and FAD 69 training areas
- 📅 Aerosport moved FROM here to Wintervogel when lease wasn't renewed (~2018)
